Custom monster variations to increase difficulty and spark some more life into PvE on your server.

# New Monster Variants

### Baby Monsters
Some new monsters now get baby variants:
* Skeleton
* Spider
* Creeper
* Enderman

### Fast Monsters (I)
These monsters run much faster than their standard counterparts while having less health. Fast skeletons will even put down the bow in favor of melee.
* Zombie
* Skeleton
* Enderman
* Piglins
* Spiders

### Brutes (II)
Brutes are large, slow, and bulky, wielding an axe.
* Zombie
* Skeleton
* Wither Skeleton

### Ghost Soldier

### Rotting Corpse (II)
It's hard to tell if these are zombies or skeletons. Likely something inbetween.

### Unstable 🧨 (II)
Unstable mobs have TNT strapped to their heads and they're ready to set it off. They'll set you on fire with flint & steel and explode on death.
* Skeleton
* Zombie

### Tarantula (III)
Large, bulky, and fast.

### Colossus (V)
These mobs are true giants, but will reward generous amounts of XP when slain.

### Combos
It's a small mob riding a larger mob. Sometimes the stack will just keep going...

# Scale Variants
Most monsters now have a chance to be spawned with an abnormal scale. This scale ranges from being much smaller to much larger. Larger mobs have more health and do more damage while moving slower, while smaller mobs move faster and deal less damage. 

# PJ's Enchants Compatibility
Some PJ's Monsters will wield weapons with custom enchantments from PJ's Enchants if the plugin is also active. 
* Ghost Soldiers use diamond swords with Phantom
* Brutes use stone axes with Fracture
[Download it here.](https://modrinth.com/plugin/pjs-enchants)
